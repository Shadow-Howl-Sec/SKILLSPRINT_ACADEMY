Optional one-time online sync (plan §6.2). The shipped resource_catalog.json lists public-domain / CC-licensed pages and PDFs (OWASP cheat sheets, NIST publications, MITRE ATT&CK technique pages, PortSwigger articles). Running the script once downloads each URL into instance/resource_cache/.html and records a CachedResource DB row, after which the link metadata service serves cached metadata forever without any outbound HTTP. 

# one-time online:
pwsh scripts\sync_resource_cache.ps1

Re-runs are safe — already-cached URLs are skipped and DB rows are upserted by original URL.
